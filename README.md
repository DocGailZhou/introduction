## About Dr. Gail Zhou

**Personal Statement**: I seek to learn, grow, understand, accept, show empathy, and contribute meaningfully to others and the world around me. Words that resonate with me: **family**, **purpose**, **innovation**, **responsibility**, **accountability**, **commitment**, **contribution**, **resilience**. 

Dr. Gail Zhou retired from Microsoft in 2026. She was a Principal Software Engineer, also held the role of Principal Software Architect. While at Microsoft, she led 60+ customer engagements, published 12 GitHub solution accelerators.

She is an inventor with two awarded US patents. LinkedIn: [Gail Zhou, PhD | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gailzhou/).

Her PhD dissertation pioneered AI/ML applications in process control, using MATLAB to simulate systems and generate thousands of training datasets for ML algorithms.
